Our Story

About Rupamata Mitra Mandal

Founded in 2005, Rupamata Mitra Mandal has been a beacon of devotion, culture, and community service in Savedi, Ahilyanagar. For over 30 years, we have been celebrating Ganesh Chaturthi with unmatched grandeur, bringing together thousands of devotees from across the region.

Our mandal stands at the intersection of tradition and modernity — preserving the sacred rituals while embracing innovation to create a world-class celebration experience for every generation.

History

Established in 2010 by passionate youth, we have grown into one of Pune's most respected Ganpati Mandals with a legacy of excellence.

🌟

Vision

A spiritually enriching, culturally vibrant, and socially responsible Ganesh Festival experience for every devotee.

🎯

Mission

Preserve tradition, promote culture, foster community bonds, and serve society through the sacred celebration of Ganesh Chaturthi.

💪

Objectives

Organize grand celebrations, support local artists, conduct charity drives, and inspire youth to connect with their cultural roots.

10 Days of Devotion

Festival Timeline

Day 1 · Sep 14

Ganpati Sathapna

Grand procession bringing Lord Ganesha home with dhol, tasha, and devotional songs through decorated streets.

Day 5 · Aug 26

Mahaprasad

Grand community feast for 100+ devotees serving authentic traditional Maharashtrian cuisine.

Days 7 - 9

Cultural Programs

Classical dance, Games, Bhajan competitions, and performances by acclaimed regional artists.

Day 10 · Sep 25

Visarjan

Grand farewell procession to the river with thousands of devotees, dhol-tasha, flowers, and music.

Days 1–10 Daily

Daily Aarti

Four-time daily Aarti with live bhajan performances and prasad distribution to all devotees.
